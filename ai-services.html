The intranet, explained

Why most companies build one

As a company grows, knowledge scatters. Policies live in inboxes, forms live on desktops, and every new hire asks the same ten questions. An intranet fixes that: one private home for the company, with a page for every department — legal, HR, sales, operations — where the current documents, processes, and people always live.

01

One source of truth

The current policy, the current form, the current price list. Nobody works off last year’s email attachment again.

02

Onboarding in days

New hires read the playbook instead of interrupting the team. HR answers each question once — not every quarter.

03

Nothing walks out the door

When someone leaves, their knowledge stays. The process was never in their head — it’s on the page.

What we can build

AI that does the work, not just the talking

Beyond the intranet, we build AI systems that plug into how your business already runs. A few examples:

01

Leads that book themselves

A lead comes in from your website, your Google profile, or a text. AI qualifies it and books the estimate straight into your sales team’s calendar. No phone tag, no lead going cold overnight.

02

Photo to proposal

The customer uploads photos of the job. AI looks at the photos, estimates the scope of work, and drafts a priced proposal for your review. You approve it; it sends. Estimates that took days take minutes.

03

Social outbound, handled

AI runs your outbound marketing on social — posts, messages, and follow-ups in your voice, aimed at the customers you actually want — while you stay on the tools.

04

The right knock at the right time

AI watches for industry triggers and reaches out with exactly the offer that fits the moment:

Trigger · Trademark filing
A business files a new trademark or copyright — their brand is changing. We reach out and offer the new website to match it.
Trigger · Unclaimed profile
A business hasn’t claimed its Google Business Profile. We point out what that’s costing them and offer to claim and build it out.
Trigger · Listing, no website
A Google listing with no website behind it. We show them the gap — and offer to fill it.
